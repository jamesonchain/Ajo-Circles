# Pitch

The two minute story starts with the problem: people already save together, but one missed payment can break the whole circle. Within the first twenty seconds, show a live circle, its next payment, and the rotating pot.

Show members joining different turns, explain that earlier turns require a larger refundable deposit, and complete a contribution. Show a missed payment being covered from that member deposit, then show the recipient receiving the full pot. End with the size of rotating savings markets and the ask to help bring reliable group saving to more communities.
