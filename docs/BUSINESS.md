# Business

Ajo and its related rotating savings traditions help people save in groups across Nigeria and many other markets. The product makes the shared commitment visible and auditable while keeping the language familiar.

Revenue is a small fee on each payout. Later revenue can include a share of yield and premium circles with additional coordination tools. The first market is Nigeria through communities, markets, and workplaces.

The main risks are missed payments, token usability, and local trust. Slot based deposits, transparent events, plain language, and a devnet first rollout reduce those risks. The product will not present yield until it is tested end to end.
